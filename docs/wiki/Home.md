# SWIVEL wiki

**SWIVEL turns an ordinary Android phone into a passive scanner that lets you *see* the Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth gadgets, trackers and cameras broadcasting around you — and keep a private record.** No hacking, no signups, nothing ever leaves your phone.

## The one rule that makes SWIVEL trustworthy

> **A match is evidence, not proof.** When SWIVEL names a maker, it means *"this is hardware made by company X."* It does **not** prove which product it is, that it's recording, or that any person or agency is present. And a device with a **randomized address is never given a maker** — SWIVEL refuses to guess. It would rather tell you less and be right.
